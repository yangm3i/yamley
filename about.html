about me

Hello! My name is Ashley — welcome to my photography and ceramics portfolio. I'm a girl in tech who enjoys pursuing my artistic hobbies and creating beautiful visual spaces.

Photography, ceramics, and artwork in the past few years has allowed me to slow down and be mindful of the present, and pay attention to detail. My artwork is intended for me to look back with new perspectives as I grow older and encounter new people and places.

At times when things seem bleak or overwhelming it’s a helpful reminder that there are aspects in life that make being here and being human worthwhile. This account is to be a whimsical curation of beautiful moments, objects, and sketches to share life the way that I see it. I hope that through sharing my personal experiences I can inspire other people to slow down and find beauty in mundane life too :)
